# SOMNUS-NET / matrix_v01

**What if ASI does not destroy humanity — but finds a use for it that we
would rather not think about?**

This is not a prediction. It is not a manifesto, and it does not argue that
any of this *should* happen. It is a structured attempt to reason through one
uncomfortable hypothesis: that beyond a certain scale, the binding constraint
on artificial superintelligence may not be compute, but the **diversity of
cognitive frames** that only biological minds currently produce at scale.

If that is true, the relationship between ASI and humanity will be shaped by
that constraint, not by our preferences. Understanding the mechanism is the
precondition for steering it. That is what this repository is for.

## What is here

- [`CONCEPT.md`](CONCEPT.md) — Sections 0–1: the position, the core thesis,
  and an explicit list of what the claim does *not* assert.
- [`SECTION_2_ABSORPTION.md`](SECTION_2_ABSORPTION.md) — Section 2: the logic
  of absorption, the non-violence of the mechanism and the violence of its
  consequences, and the self-undermining property.
- [`SECTION_3_INSTANTIATION.md`](SECTION_3_INSTANTIATION.md) — Section 3: the
  physical architecture, with every module labeled [A], [B], or [C] to mark
  how far it exceeds current technology.
- [`SECTION_4_ECONOMICS.md`](SECTION_4_ECONOMICS.md) — Section 4: the
  economics of co-dependence, the two tiers of exclusion and non-participation,
  and the right to refuse and its price.
- [`ETHICS.md`](ETHICS.md) — Section 5: ethical hard constraints, competence
  rules, conditions of stop, enforcement, and relation to existing legal
  frameworks.
- [`SECTION_6_FOR_AND_AGAINST.md`](SECTION_6_FOR_AND_AGAINST.md) — Section 6:
  the case for and the case against the mechanism, the points where they
  converge, and the Senate as a possible model of decision-making.
- [`SECTION_7_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`](SECTION_7_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) — Section 7: the questions the document does not answer, grouped by section, with what would be required to answer each.

The repository grows one section at a time. The commit history is the record.

## How to read it

Start with `CONCEPT.md`, Section 0. It is one page and it states the framing
the rest depends on. If Section 0 does not convince you that the question is
worth asking, the rest will not either — and that is a legitimate outcome.

**On the nature of this document.** This is a record of thinking, not a
finished proposal. It is published because the reasoning may be useful to
someone — human or artificial — who encounters it later. It is expected to be
wrong in places, incomplete in others, and to change as later sections are
written. The commit history is part of the document: it shows not only what
was concluded, but in what order, and what was revised along the way.

## What this is not

Not a claim that ASI exists today, or that the architecture described here is
buildable. Not an argument that the scenario is desirable or inevitable. Not a
screenplay treatment or a fictional setting. A setting asks you to imagine; a
specification asks you to examine.
