# Authorship and Origin

This document records who created the concept, how it was developed, and
what each party contributed. It exists because a record of thinking is only
useful if it is clear whose thinking it records, and under what conditions
it may be used.

## AI assistance

The text of this repository was developed in dialogue with an AI model
(DeepSeek). The model assisted with structuring, drafting, and
editing. All decisions about content, scope, framing, and final approval
were made by the author. The model is not an author in the legal sense and
does not hold rights to the work.

This disclosure is included because a record of thinking is only useful if
it is honest about how the thinking was produced. The model's contribution
was real and is acknowledged. The author's contribution was decisive and is
recorded above.

## How the document was developed

1. The author formulated the initial concept and its core thesis.
2. The author and the model worked through the structure section by section,
   with the author making every decision about what to include, what to
   exclude, and how to frame it.
3. Each section was written, reviewed, and committed separately. The commit
   history records the order of development and the revisions made along the
   way.
4. Ethical constraints were established early and applied to every
   subsequent section.
